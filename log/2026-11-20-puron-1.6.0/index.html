puron 1.6.0

Significant UX improvements, and new flatpak support.

Changes
+Puron is also built as a Flatpak, with the app ID io.github.elalonde.Puron, for the COSMIC Flatpak repository.
*Each pinned temperature on the panel shows an icon for its kind of device, such as the CPU, the graphics card, a drive, the Wi-Fi card, a wired network card or a memory module, so that two pinned temperatures can be told apart by the icon alone.
*Puron has its own app icon. COSMIC Settings shows it for the applet in every package, and the panel shows its single-color form when nothing is pinned.
